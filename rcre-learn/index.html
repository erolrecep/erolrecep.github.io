The Silicon Grid & The Locker Room

Welcome to the physical machine! Computer RAM is like an endless school hallway of numbered metal lockers. Each locker holds exactly one byte (0–255). In C23, variables are just sticky notes on locker doors, and pointers are cards telling you which locker number to open.
